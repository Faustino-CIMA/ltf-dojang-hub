# LTF Dojang Hub 0.13.1

3 November 2026

The user manual for 0.13.0 is updated. Screenshots were recaptured, the language menu is described, and the PDFs were rebuilt in English, French, German, and Luxembourgish.

There is no other change in how the app works, and there is no database migration. The manuals and PDFs do not need a deploy. The version shown in the sidebar and on About moves from 0.13.0 to 0.13.1. That is the only application change. A deploy is what makes that number appear.

## Changed

- **User manual.** The 0.13.0 manual, in four languages, includes the recaptured screenshots and the language menu. The PDFs were rebuilt.
