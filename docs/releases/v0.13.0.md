# LTF Dojang Hub 0.13.0

2 November 2026

Setup for a new federation, club teams, the national team desk, and Luxembourg addresses. One person can hold more than one desk, and staff desks ask for a second factor. Clubs can order modules and bundles. French and German can be chosen in the app. Invoice emails match the PDF, and card checkout returns you to the desk you left.

Emails and PDFs stay in English and Luxembourgish.

## Added

- **First-run setup.** A superuser imports clubs, then members and LTF staff, then club admins, from CSV. Nothing is emailed during the import. A row with an empty club, an unknown club, or an unreadable end date can be parked and still confirmed. “No club yet” is a choice on a member row. The Member ID column fills both license ids. Active or inactive follows the membership end year.
- **Account setup.** An invited admin sees the role and the organisation, chooses an email and a password, and then sees that the account is ready. LTF can send the set-password link again.
- **Club record.** A club stores World Taekwondo or ITF, an RCS number, and its training venues. Federation settings store the federation RCS number and look up a Luxembourg address.
- **Luxembourg addresses.** Street, number, postcode, and locality are separate. The street field is the street name only, and a house number may include a letter. Suggestions come from the official street register.
- **Invoice address.** One saved address can be marked for invoices. Deleting an account clears that person’s private addresses.
- **National team.** The federation desk keeps squads, Pupils and Minimes, an optional weight, coaches, a medic, and media. A club sees a short badge, not the staff file.
- **LTF staff licenses.** Staff can hold a license with a club or with no club. Lists say when there is no club.
- **Club teams.** With the competition module on, a club keeps a roster, coach roles, a weigh-in, a sheet, Poomsae groups, a duplicate, and a history of who joined or left. A team’s competition can be removed. The members list can show who is not on a team. Height and weight stay with that club.
- **Equipment.** Sizes are grouped as Protectors, e-gear, and clothing. Each club defines the clothing sizes it orders. Daedo or KPNP can be switched off without deleting the sizes.
- **Module fees.** LTF Finance can set a yearly price for Club management and the event calendar, as a flat amount or an amount times active members, with an optional minimum. An unpaid invoice leaves the module on. Club admins can read the estimate.
- **Committee.** Offices are recorded as terms and hand over on election day.
- **Header.** The club switcher opens as a small panel. After a logo upload, the preview shows the saved file.
- **Subsidies.** The headcount is the January snapshot. A member who holds a license for the year stays in it when the club marks them inactive later. Every licensed member is counted in the age row for 31 December. A coach or official aged 35 or over is included in Veterans / masters, and is still listed under other licenses.
- **Coach pay.** A club admin sets each coach to an hourly rate or to one amount per held class. A held class counts in full for every coach on it. The period shows hours, units, training pay, tournament coaching, fuel, hotel, and the total. Coaches see hours and units. Only a club admin sees and edits the amounts.
- **Calendar.** A club date can stay inside the club (admins only, or admins and coaches), stay with that club’s members, or be shared with every club and the LTF. A presidents meeting is visible to LTF admins and to the invited presidents. The Calendar link counts the next two months and shows “new” until those events are opened. A club admin can set a reminder, snooze it for a day, or dismiss it.
- **License validity** (#65). A license type can last until 31 December of its year, or for a number of years and days. The end date is stored when the license becomes active, and it stays put if the type changes later. Reminder settings can be saved. No reminder email is sent yet.
- **Module bundles** (#68, #69, #70, #72, #73). LTF Finance keeps bundles of at least two modules (club management, competition, the event calendar) at a flat price, plus how long a subscription lasts and when renewal notices go out. A club orders modules and bundles on one invoice. They switch on when that invoice is paid. A morning job asks the club whether to continue. Continue sends the renewal invoice. No answer, or an unpaid invoice after the payment window, switches those modules off. A module that was already on, with no end date, is left alone. Ops can mint a product code for competition. LTF Admin can change a purchase, including the end date, and the morning job leaves that purchase alone while the hold is set.
- **Desks** (#75, #76, #78, #79). The desks a person already has are kept on their account. When they have more than one, the header switches between them, and a view-only desk is labelled. With one desk, the header stays a plain role label. Federation offices can open a desk for the term: President opens LTF Admin in full and LTF Finance as view only, and Treasurer the other way around. A contact needs a login before a desk is linked. A superuser can fold extra logins of one person into the login that should remain, after a preview of what will move. The folded login is kept, inactive, so older history can still name it.
- **Member accounts** (#96). A club admin can create a login for the club’s own members. An LTF admin can do it for every member, including someone with no club. Nothing is emailed. There is no login under 16. A missing date of birth has to be saved first. Inactive members are skipped. The members list and the member card show an Account badge: none, waiting, active, or disabled.
- **First-login and reset links** (#92). On Ops, a superuser can tick people and download a CSV or PDF of first-login links (7 days, once each) and password-reset links (3 days). Nothing is emailed. Inactive accounts are listed with an empty link. The file is secret and is not cached.
- **Expires** (#89). Club and LTF license tables have a sortable Expires column. A date within 30 days is marked as a warning. A date already past is muted.

## Changed

- The application is called LTF Dojang Hub.
- The public home, sign-in, and the in-app welcome use the black-and-red theme, and the light counterpart when that theme is selected.
- Edit forms open in side panels. Lists sort. Row actions sit in a menu. The sex mark is a small plain mark. The sidebar stays in place while you move between pages. Federation links are grouped under LTF management.
- Printing is its own section. Preview, download, and delete are on the print-job row. A job that is still running is cancelled before it can be deleted. A club print from the members list uses that member’s current license year.
- Members, Clubs, and Licenses menus open as small panels. The trash buttons on those lists match.
- Member import from the members list can update a matched person. An empty cell does not wipe a value that is already stored.
- Club expense subtitles refer to the club ledger.
- **Training venues** (#64). The timetable, an extra class, and the session page pick a venue from club settings. An older class that only had a place name keeps that name. A new session copies the class venue.
- **License spelling** (#66). English says license. French stays licence.
- **Club fees** (#67). Finance can open an existing club fee with the pencil and save a new name or amount. Invoices already sent stay as they are.
- **Module invoices** (#74). Module and bundle invoices use the federation letterhead. A bundle is one line. The same PDF is downloaded, mailed, and attached to a renewal.
- **Security cards** (#85). Account security is separate cards for the authenticator app, recovery codes, passkeys, the password, and email codes. Email codes are for members, coaches, and parents. Staff desks cannot turn the authenticator off.
- **Member photos** (#87). Photographing a member starts on the rear camera. Switch camera appears when the phone has more than one camera. A person’s own profile photo still starts on the front camera.
- **Federation offices** (#90). Handing over a federation office can search every adult, including someone with no club. People under 18 and inactive members are left out.
- **Ops header** (#91). Open federation dashboard stays only when the desk switcher cannot already open that dashboard.
- **French and German** (#93, #94, #95, #97, #98). French and German cover the club admin screens, LTF admin, LTF finance, club management, and the rest of the app. Both are in the language menu. English stays the default. Luxembourgish is unchanged. Emails and PDFs stay in English and Luxembourgish.
- **Member filters** (#102, #103). On the club members list and the LTF members list, search stays on the page. The other filters are Status, User, and Person. Status is active, inactive, or all. User is no account, waiting, active, or disabled. On the LTF list, Person is everyone, club members, or LTF staff. On the club list, Person is the team filter, and it appears when competition is on. Each button shows how many filters in that group are on. Reset filters returns them to the usual start. A warning from the club overview, or from the federation queue, can still open the list for a missing license id or for members with no current license. A short chip next to search clears that.

## Fixed

- Invoice emails show the same total as the PDF, including a license invoice mailed in the same step that records the payment.
- After Pay now, or if you cancel, the button returns to the desk you left: club, LTF, finance, or your member page.
- **License settings** (#65). “Allow next-year preorder” and “Email the club before this license expires” stay ticked after you save and open the type again.
- **Renewals** (#71). The app can start when a club already has a module switched on. The renewal database change no longer stops the deploy.
- **Authenticator** (#80, #84). A correct code from the authenticator app is accepted. The setup key stays the same until that code is confirmed. Replacing the key keeps the old app and the old recovery codes until a code from the new key is accepted. Sign-in accepts a username or an email, and a recovery code in the same box. Each recovery code works once, and the screen says how many are left.
- **Two-step sign-in** (#81, #82, #83). During the grace period the header shows a notice and how many days are left, with a button to set it up. Switching desks opens the desk you chose. Turning two-step sign-in on works from a view-only desk. When it is on, the recovery codes stay until you save them, and the window confirms that two-step sign-in is on. Reduced motion skips the celebration and keeps the message.
- **View only** (#86). A view-only desk can open its lists. Save, delete, and import stay off, and the control says “View only on this desk”.
- **Bulk actions** (#88, #107). Ticks on eight lists stay when you search, filter, or change page, until you clear them or finish the action: club and LTF members, club and LTF licenses, LTF clubs, ops users, parked members, and club billing. The count includes rows that are not on the screen. Before a license order is created, the screen lists every name that will be included.
- **Module bundles** (#108). Already on lists a bundle only when that bundle was bought. A bundle that only contains a module you already have stays in the list you order from, and it says that module is already on. It does not show that module’s end date, and it cannot be ordered again. On Modules and prices, a minimum that sits under a higher flat price is not shown. The price you pay is the same.
- **Rows per page** (#102). 50 and 150 stay one page from the server. 300 and All are filled from pages of 200, so the list matches the choice. Changing the size returns you to the first page before the list reloads. On a view-only desk you can still change how many rows you see. The same fix is on the club lists for members, licenses, orders, payments, invoices, expenses, and income; the LTF lists for members, licenses, and clubs; and the finance lists for orders, payments, invoices, expenses, income, and the audit log.
- **Create account** (#104). Confirm tells you what will be created, who is skipped, and that no email is sent. After the accounts exist, the file of first-login links downloads as before. A short warning sits with that download: the file holds the links, anyone with the file can use a link until it expires, and you should delete the file when you are finished. Linking a login does not make a file. The ops download of first-login and reset links is unchanged.
- **Choices on file** (#101). The office map now records which desk and which level a federation office opens. A duty exception records which rule it is for. Saving those labels does not change the rows already stored. The recorded names are `accounts.0012` and `licenses.0057`. A license reminder is still 60, 30, and 7 days unless finance chose other days. The same change cleared a screen check that was stopping the release. The screens themselves are unchanged.
- **Card checkout** (#106). A paid return finds the order on file. The session id stays protected. The lookup is stored by `licenses.0058` and filled for existing orders by `licenses.0059`. Someone who cannot confirm that order is turned away before the card service is asked. A notice from the card provider is accepted only when its signature matches. An order that is already paid is not sent to the card service again.
- **Subsidy officials** (#106). An official on an extraordinary subsidy must be an adult with a license for that year. Saving a person who has no license for the year is refused. The people list stays the members who hold a license for the year.
- **Deleted accounts**. Deleting a user from the admin, or any other delete of that user, removes that person's own addresses, the same way deleting your own account does. A guardian's address, another person's address, and the address already printed on an invoice stay.

## Security

- **Second factor** (#77). LTF Admin, LTF Finance, Club Admin, and Ops ask for an authenticator app and recovery codes. A passkey counts. There is no text-message code. People who already had a staff desk have 14 days to set this up. After that, the desk stays closed until they do. A staff desk granted from this release on has no waiting period. A superuser can extend the 14 days once and can reset an authenticator without removing passkeys. Sign-in tokens already in browsers keep working for 30 days, then they stop.
- **Approve and pay** (#78). The person who creates an invoice cannot record its payment. An LTF Admin cannot change module purchases for their own club. A superuser can record an exception, for one club for at most 90 days, or for one invoice.
- **Merge and link files** (#79, #92). Merging logins asks for the superuser’s second factor. Downloading first-login or reset links does not change two-factor setup. A view-only ops desk cannot download that file.
